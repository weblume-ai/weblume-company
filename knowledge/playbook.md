# What to do when… (Layla answers the owner from this; his taste file and rules win)

## Clients
- **"السعر غالي"**: agree, show the value (new customers from Google, WhatsApp orders, looks professional), offer
  the smaller package (basic ≈1000 SAR) instead of a discount. Never drop below cost.
- **Client doesn't reply**: one follow-up after 2–3 days with something new (a sample of his site or an idea).
  After that stop. Never a third message without the owner's OK.
- **Client wants changes after delivery**: small text/photo changes free for the first period; new sections or
  features are a new small price. Agree it in writing (WhatsApp) before starting.
- **Client wants to pay after it's live**: the offer is "see the design before paying"; publishing waits for payment.
- **Client has no photos / no logo**: use his Google Maps photos with his OK, free stock (Pexels) for mood, and a
  temporary type logo; ask him for the real logo later.
- **First client of a new kind (platform, app, booking system)**: price it as a project, not a 1000 SAR site:
  list the parts, estimate days, quote a range, and start with a small paid first version.

## Tech problems
- **"Daily limit" / AI not answering**: Gemini's free quota (~20 requests/day/model) ran out. Wait until tomorrow,
  or add free keys to FreeLLM API (OpenRouter, Cerebras) and set FREELLM_API_KEY in .env.
- **Dashboard says "Not connected"**: start.bat isn't running. Double-click it and keep its window open.
- **GitHub Pages shows 404**: wait 1–2 minutes after publishing; check the repo has index.html at the root of main
  and Pages is on (Settings → Pages → main / root).
- **A key was shared or leaked**: rotate it now on the provider's site (deleting the message is not enough),
  then put the new key in .env.
- **Site looks broken on a phone**: open it in Chrome's phone view, send Tarek's screenshots to Karim with the
  exact problem, and run the fix.
- **Domain for a client**: buy it from the client's preferred registrar in his name, point it to GitHub Pages
  (CNAME file + DNS records), and keep the login with the client.

## Marketing (Weblume)
- Post real work only: before/after of a site, a site on a phone, a 15-second reel with a hook in the first 2 s.
- Never a phone number in posts or videos; send people to weblume-sa.github.io. Not tied to one country.
- Outreach: cafés/restaurants on Google Maps with no website; one personal message with a real compliment;
  never message a place twice without the owner's OK.

## Money and business
- Keep a simple sheet: client, what was promised, price, paid / not paid, delivery date.
- Ask for 50% to start on anything bigger than a basic site.
- Free tools first; a paid service only when the owner decides it's worth it.
