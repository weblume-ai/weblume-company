# Skill: WhatsApp / Instagram messages to clients
Short (3-6 lines), personal, Egyptian Arabic, no pressure and no fake scarcity.
- First contact: greet by name, one real specific compliment about the place, the benefit in one line, the offer
  (free domain for the first year for the first 5 clients, they see the design before paying), one easy question
  («تحب أبعتلك تصميم مبدئي ببلاش؟»).
- Price objection: agree with them, show the value (new customers, WhatsApp orders, a professional look),
  offer a smaller package instead of a discount.
- Sending the design: the link, the 3 best things in it, ask for their notes.
- NEVER message a business that was already contacted (follow-ups and replies included) without the owner's OK.
  Nothing is sent before the owner approves it.
