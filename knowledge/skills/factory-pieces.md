# Skill: choose the factory pieces
The page is built from tested pieces; you only choose ids (one per type) from the PIECES list you are given.
- No client photos doesn't mean no photos: the factory fetches matching stock photos and footage for every site (client
  uploads first). Pick photo pieces normally; choose no-photo pieces only for places too unusual for stock.
- Always: nav, a hero, a menu, visit, footer-huge, bar. Add about, features, band, cta, gallery when they help the idea.
- Hero by what media there is: client video → hero-frames (scroll-played footage, the strongest); 2+ cut-out
  products → hero-showcase or hero-stage; 3+ dish photos → hero-ring; one good photo → hero-split, hero-arch,
  hero-name-photo, hero-blueprint (lines draw a blueprint, a window opens on the photo: cinematic); nothing →
  hero-waterline or hero-words.
- Menu: 4+ dish photos → menu-rail, menu-tiles, menu-iso, menu-polaroid, menu-flip, menu-rows; no photos → menu-cols.
- Features (only with 2+ real strengths): features-glow, features-bento, features-scrub (needs a video or photo).
- Gallery (3+ photos): gallery-track, gallery-sheet, gallery-stack, gallery-curve (a curved wall in a dark room).
- About: about-say, about-statement, about-badge, about-bubbles, about-labels (text in black labels, light page).
- Mood: luxury/cinematic → dark pages, hero-frames, menu-rail, features-scrub, cta-glow. Playful → hero-stage,
  menu-iso, band-stripes, about-bubbles. Simple/modern → hero-split, menu-rows, about-statement.
- Variety: neighbouring sections must look different (don't put two dark photo-heavy pieces in a row), and each new
  site should not repeat the last site's hero.
If you're unsure a piece's needs are met, pick the simpler piece: the factory swaps a piece whose media is missing,
but a good choice beats a fallback.
