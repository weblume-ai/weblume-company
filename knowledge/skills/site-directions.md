# Skill: pick a direction before pieces (templates, motion and voice that belong together)
A site looks designed when one idea decides the layout, the movement and the words together. Before choosing pieces,
pick ONE direction below that fits the place, write it in the brief as `direction`, and let it decide everything.
Never the direction of the last site. (Ideas adapted from the MIT-licensed tasteskill library of gallery sites.)

1. **The name as a monument**: the name so big it's architecture, cropped by the screen edge, one small line under it.
   Pieces: hero-wordmark, marquee-band, footer-huge, menu-rows. Motion: the name scales a little as you scroll;
   one line of huge type travels sideways between sections. Voice: confident, very few words, the name says it.
2. **The page is the object**: build the site as the thing the place already prints: a restaurant is its menu, a
   bakery is its box label, a café is its cup sleeve or receipt. Pieces: hero-film-title or hero-words, menu-flip /
   menu-polaroid, about-labels, steps-order. Motion: pages turn, labels stamp in, receipts unroll. Voice: written like
   the object (menu headings, receipt lines «١ × كنافة … ٤٥»).
3. **One motif**: one shape from the place runs everything (the café's awning stripes, the bakery's round tray, the
   juice bar's straw). Pieces: hero-ring or hero-orbit, band-stripes, about-floats, cta-glow. Motion: the motif draws,
   spins or repeats as the section enters. Voice: playful, one running joke or word that repeats.
4. **The object, large**: one product photo bigger than seems reasonable, cropped by the screen. Needs a good
   cut-out or photo. Pieces: hero-showcase / hero-stage, menu-sticky-photo, gallery-stack. Motion: the product
   rotates or rises as you scroll, the picture settles from 1.12 to 1. Voice: short sensory lines («مقرمشة من بره»).
5. **The specimen catalogue**: every item alone on its own flat colour, same size, same angle, named plainly, like a
   field guide. Perfect for bakeries, sweets, juices. Pieces: menu-tiles or menu-iso, band-chapters, features-bento.
   Motion: tiles pop in one after another; each chapter changes the page colour. Voice: name + 3 plain words.
6. **Colour chapters**: each section a full flat colour from the brand, like posters hung in a row. Pieces:
   band-chapters, hero-split, menu-cols, about-statement, cta-blur. Motion: the ground changes exactly at section
   edges; headings slide in. Voice: one bold claim per chapter.
7. **The place carries it**: for a place people visit, the photograph is the argument: one full-screen photo, the
   name small along the bottom like a film title. Pieces: hero-frames or hero-film-title, how-film, gallery-curve,
   visit. Motion: slow Ken Burns, a window that opens onto the photo. Voice: calm and few words, the mood not a list.
8. **The split stage**: half the screen holds one photo still while the other half scrolls the facts. Pieces:
   hero-split, menu-sticky-photo, about-say. Motion: the held photo swaps when its section arrives. Voice: practical,
   clear, hours and prices easy to find.
9. **The dark room**: one dark page where photos glow like exhibits. For night places, dessert bars, luxury. Pieces:
   hero-blueprint or hero-card-room, menu-rail, gallery-curve, cta-glow. Motion: each photo lights up as it arrives,
   slow and quiet. Voice: low, short, a little poetic. (Never dark-with-gold.)
10. **Loud and flat**: street-poster directness, flat saturated colour, the heaviest type, cut-out photos stuck on.
    For burgers, juices, young brands. Pieces: hero-stage, band-stripes, menu-iso, about-bubbles, marquee-band.
    Motion: things slam and bounce in. Voice: what a person would shout across the street («جعان؟ تعالى»).

Rules for every direction:
- Every site moves (Asser's rule): at least three pieces that move on their own, and one signature movement from the
  direction above that the visitor remembers.
- Words: headlines 3-6 words in the client's dialect, one idea per section, buttons are verbs with an outcome.
  The direction's voice wins over a generic tone; never "أفضل جودة"، "خدمة مميزة" or other filler.
- Problems: if a piece's media is missing, swap within the same direction (another piece from its list) rather than
  falling back to a generic piece; if the photos are weak, pick a direction that needs fewer (1, 3, 6, 10).
