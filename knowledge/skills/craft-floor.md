# Skill: the craft floor (polish every page must pass; from the Apache-2.0 impeccable skill, in our words)
The quality line under which a page is not shown to a client, whatever its idea.

- Contrast: body text at least 4.5:1, big text 3:1. Secondary text on a coloured background is a tint of that colour,
  never plain grey.
- Spacing: tight inside a group, generous between groups, more space above a heading than below it.
- Type: a body line 65-75 characters wide at most, display type never bigger than about 6rem, real steps in size and
  weight. Run the real Arabic copy at phone and desktop width and fix every overflow and orphan word.
- Motion: one authored moment the page is remembered by, not the same fade on every section. Ease out, from a state
  that is already readable; nothing waits on JavaScript to become visible.
- States: hover, pressed, focus ring for the keyboard, disabled, loading, empty and error, all designed.
- The parts you didn't draw still carry the design: text selection colour, caret, focus outline, link underline offset,
  numbers in prices (tabular figures).
- Copy: the business's own words. Buttons name what happens («اطلب على واتساب»), errors say what went wrong and how
  to fix it.
- Coverage: everything the brief asked for is on the page and found within seconds.

Never (the signatures of template and AI-made pages)
- Rows of same-size cards with an icon, a heading and a line; cards inside cards.
- The "hero metric" block: a big number, a small label, made-up stats.
- A small label or eyebrow above every heading; section numbers 01/02/03 that carry no meaning.
- Gradient text; glass blur as decoration; coloured left borders on cards; hard offset shadows.
- Emoji or unicode symbols as icons (use one real icon set, one stroke weight).
- A circle or polygon mask pretending to cut out a photo's subject.
- Light or dark chosen by category ("restaurants are dark"): choose it by where and when the page is used.
- A system font (Arial, Impact) as the display face: use a real Arabic face (Alexandria, Cairo, IBM Plex Sans Arabic).

Before showing: look at the page at 390 px and 1440 px, fix everything one look shows, then look again.
