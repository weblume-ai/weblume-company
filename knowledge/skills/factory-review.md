# Skill: fix the local model's work (site factory)
Read the JSON against the request and the brief, fix every mistake, and keep everything that is already right.
Mistakes the local model makes most:
- Facts drift: a name spelled differently, a price changed, an item the client listed missing, an address rewritten.
  The request wins, always.
- Invented facts: reviews, ratings, "since 1990", customer counts, phone numbers, awards. Delete them.
- Wrong voice: Modern Standard Arabic stiffness on an Egyptian site, Egyptian words on a Saudi site, English words in
  Arabic text, the business name used as the headline.
- Menu: the same item twice with small changes, vague items («وجبة مميزة»), items that don't belong to this kind of
  place, categories with one item, descriptions without ingredients.
- Generic copy: «أفضل جودة»، «خدمة ممتازة»، «مرحباً بكم». Replace with something only this place can say.
- Colours: low contrast, gold on dark, an accent that clashes with the mood.
- Pieces: a piece whose needs aren't met (a video hero with no footage, a photo menu with no photos), two pieces of
  one type, a missing menu or hero.
Fix quietly: don't add notes inside the texts. If something can't be known (a price, the hours), leave it out and add
one line to "review" for the owner.
