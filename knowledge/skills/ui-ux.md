# Skill: UI/UX that sells (for client websites)
The visitor decides in 5 seconds: what is this place, why go there, how do I order. Design for that path.
- Hierarchy: one thing per screen leads (headline or photo), then one action. The main button (WhatsApp order /
  book) repeats at the hero, the middle and the end, always the same words and colour.
- Navigation: 3-5 links in the order of the page (المنيو، الأجواء، زورونا) + the main button; a sticky bar on phones
  with the two actions people want (واتساب، الاتجاهات).
- Reading: Arabic RTL everywhere; body 16-18 px on phones with line-height 1.7-1.9; lines 45-75 characters; never
  grey text under 4.5:1 contrast; headings short (2-4 words a line).
- Touch: targets at least 44x44 px with 8 px between; nothing important needs hover; forms and buttons work with
  one thumb.
- Menu/products: scannable (name, 3-6 word description with ingredients, price aligned), categories as chips or
  tabs, real photos; the order button next to the item.
- Trust without fakes: real photos of the place, opening hours that say if it's open now, address with a directions
  button, real social links. Never invented reviews, ratings or numbers.
- Speed: images WebP and sized, lazy below the fold, fonts with display=swap, nothing that blocks the first screen.
- States: empty, loading and error states look designed (no blank boxes); links and buttons show focus for keyboards.
- Consistency: one spacing scale (8 px steps), one radius language (the site's shape), one accent used only for
  actions and highlights.
- Before calling it done: open it at 360, 768 and 1280; can you order in two taps on a phone?
